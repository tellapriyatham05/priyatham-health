# Changelog

All notable changes to Priyatham Health.

## [1.1] · 2026-09-27
### Fixed
- **Laptop screen time now shows up right after adding a laptop.** Insights reloads any device whose data is older than a minute. Previously a newly added laptop stayed blank until a manual refresh.
- Laptop totals now count every app. Short uses under a minute were dropped before. The app list shows apps used for 30 s or more.

### Changed
- The refresh button is now a clear **Refresh** chip (it looked like a clock) and shows **Syncing** while it works.
- Each device card shows **"synced h:mm"**, or the reason it couldn't connect.
- Adding a laptop opens Insights and syncs immediately.

## [1.0] · 2026-09-27
### Added
- First release: Today timeline and day score, real alarms (ring / vibrate on silent / full-screen), weekly meal planner with backward-timed prep alarms, recipes, calories & protein log, workout routines with a voice-coach timer, GPS cycling, water, sleep check-in, weight & BMI, phone screen time, and laptop screen time via ActivityWatch.
- Brand identity: black · white · muted gold, and the "P in a progress ring" logo.
