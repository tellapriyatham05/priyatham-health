1 · How everything connects

Everything is stored on your phone. There is no account, no cloud and no sign-in. The laptops only share screen-time data with your phone when both are on your home Wi-Fi.

2Install the app on your OnePlus phone

The app is a file called PriyathamHealth-v1.0.apk. It's in your Google Drive and in Documents\PriyathamHealth on this laptop.

  1. On your phone, open the Google Drive app and find PriyathamHealth-v1.0.apk (use the search bar).
  2. Tap it, then tap Download (or the ⋮ menu → Download). When it finishes, tap the download notification, or open it from the Files app → Downloads.
  3. Android says "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source." Tap Settings → turn on Allow from this source → go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect shows "Unsafe app blocked" or "App scan recommended", tap More details → Install anyway. This appears because the app isn't from the Play Store. It's your own app.
  5. Tap Open. You'll see the Priyatham Health logo, then the welcome screen.
TipAfter installing, you can switch Allow from this source back off for Drive/Files (Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps).

3First-time setup

  1. Welcome screen: check your name (Priyatham), then add sex, age, height, weight and goal weight. Set your wake-up, sleep, work and workout times. Every reminder is timed from these.
  2. Set up your phone: tap Allow on each row until all show On. Some rows open an Android settings page. Switch the app on there, then press Back.
  3. Tap Test alarm. In 5 seconds it rings with the full-screen alarm. Put the phone on silent and test again: it vibrates instead of ringing.
  4. Tap Start using the app. Your water, stand-break, workout, bedtime, check-in, weekly planning and weigh-in reminders are now scheduled.

4OnePlus battery settings (don't skip)

OxygenOS closes background apps aggressively to save battery, and that can silence reminders. Do all three:

  1. Settings → Apps → App management → Priyatham Health → Battery (or Battery usage) → choose Unrestricted / Allow background activity. Also turn on Allow auto-launch if you see it.
  2. Settings → Battery → More settings → Optimise battery use: find Priyatham Health and set it to Don't optimise. (The in-app "Ignore battery optimisation" button does this too.)
  3. Open Recent apps, press and hold (or swipe down on) the Priyatham Health card and tap Lock. A lock icon appears, so "Clear all" won't close it.
If reminders ever stopOpen the app once. It re-schedules everything on launch and after every phone restart. Then re-check the three settings above, because OnePlus system updates sometimes reset them.

5Set up ActivityWatch on your laptops

ActivityWatch is free and open source. It records which apps and websites you use on a computer and keeps the data on that computer. Do these steps on laptop 1, then repeat them on laptop 2.

5a · Install it

  1. Go to activitywatch.net → Download → Windows (the installer .exe).
  2. Run the installer and accept the defaults. Tick start on login if asked, so it runs every time the laptop starts.
  3. Start ActivityWatch from the Start menu. A small ActivityWatch icon appears near the clock (click ^ to see hidden icons).
  4. Check it works: open http://localhost:5600 in a browser. You should see the ActivityWatch dashboard. After a few minutes of use it shows your apps.
Optional: website detailInstall the ActivityWatch Web Watcher extension for Chrome/Edge (linked on activitywatch.net) to see time per website, not only per browser.

5b · Let your phone read it (home Wi-Fi only)

By default ActivityWatch only answers the laptop itself. These steps let your phone ask for today's totals over your home Wi-Fi.

  1. Quit ActivityWatch: right-click the tray icon → Quit ActivityWatch.
  2. Open its config file. Press Win + R, paste the line below and press Enter: 
    notepad %LOCALAPPDATA%\activitywatch\activitywatch\aw-server-rust\config.toml
    If Notepad says the file doesn't exist, click Yes to create it. (If the folder is missing, search File Explorer's AppData for config.toml inside an aw-server-rust folder.)
  3. Make the file contain these two lines, change any existing address line, then Save: 
    # let my phone connect over home Wi-Fi
address = "0.0.0.0"
port = 5600
  4. Allow it through the firewall. Right-click Start → Terminal (Admin) or Windows PowerShell (Admin), paste this and press Enter: 
    New-NetFirewallRule -DisplayName "ActivityWatch (home Wi-Fi)" -Direction Inbound -Protocol TCP -LocalPort 5600 -Action Allow -Profile Private
  5. Mark your home Wi-Fi as Private: Settings → Network & internet → Wi-Fi → click your network → Network profile type: Private.
  6. Start ActivityWatch again from the Start menu.
  7. Find the laptop's IP address: press Win + R → type cmd → Enter → type ipconfig → Enter. Under Wireless LAN adapter Wi-Fi, note the IPv4 Address, e.g. 192.168.1.20.
  8. Test from your phone: open Chrome on the phone (on the same Wi-Fi) and go to http://192.168.1.20:5600 (your IP). If the dashboard opens, it's working.
Privacy noteThe firewall rule only opens port 5600 on Private networks, so on café or office Wi-Fi (Public) the laptop stays closed. On your home Wi-Fi, other devices could read this laptop's activity summary too, so only do this on a network you trust.
Keep the IP from changingHome routers sometimes give a laptop a new IP. In your router's admin page (often 192.168.1.1 or 192.168.0.1), look for DHCP reservation / Address reservation and reserve the IP for each laptop. If you skip this and the laptop stops syncing, run ipconfig again and update the IP in the app.

6Connect both laptops in the app

  1. In the app, open Insights (bottom right) → tap + Connect your 2 laptops (or Settings → Laptops).
  2. Name: Laptop 1 (or something like "Work laptop"). IP: the IPv4 address from step 5b. Port: 5600. Tap Test & add.
  3. You should see "Connected to <laptop name>". Repeat for Laptop 2.
  4. Back on Insights, pull the refresh button. Screen time now combines your phone + laptop 1 + laptop 2, split into work, leisure and other.

Weekend planning ritual

Every Saturday 6 pm an alarm reminds you to plan next week. Open Meals → Next week, fill the 21 slots (or Copy previous week), and check the Shopping list. That's it: every soak, grind and marinate alarm for the week is scheduled for you.

How prep alarms work (dosa example)

Your dosa recipe is: soak rice & urad dal 6 h → grind → ferment 6 h = 12 h. For Monday 8:00 am breakfast, with bedtime 11 pm and wake-up 7 am, the app counts back from the meal. Grinding at 2 am would fall in your sleep, so it moves grinding to before bed:

StepFirst alarmFinal callThen
Soak rice & urad dalSun 1:45 pmSun 4:45 pm6 h soaking
Grind the batterSun 7:45 pmSun 10:45 pmferments overnight (6 h+)
Make dosaMon 8:00 am, batter ready

Until you tap Done, the alarm repeats every 15 minutes and gets more urgent ("2 h 30 min left…", "40 min left before the final call…") until the FINAL CALL. If you miss it, Today and Prep tasks show it in red with a Change meal button. Edit any recipe's steps and times in Meals → Recipes.

9Backups and updates

  • Backup: Settings → Copy backup, then paste it into a note or an email to yourself. On a new phone: Settings → Restore → paste.
  • Updates: install a newer PriyathamHealth-vX.apk over the old one, and your data stays. Every version is signed with your private key, stored on this laptop at C:\Users\priya\PriyathamHealth\android\app\priyatham-release.jks. Keep that folder safe: Android only accepts updates signed with the same key.

Good to know
  • Calorie and protein values are typical home-cooked estimates. Edit any food or create your own in Food → Add → Create a food.
  • Ride calories use your weight and speed (standard MET values), so treat them as good estimates. Workout calories are rougher.
  • Your targets: a gentle calorie deficit (about 0.25–0.5 kg a week), protein ≈ 1.6 g per kg of goal weight, water ≈ 33 ml per kg plus 500 ml on active days.
  • This app gives general wellness guidance, not medical advice. See a doctor for anything that worries you.
